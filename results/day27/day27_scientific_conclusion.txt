Day 27 falsified the pre-registered positive historical lexical-instability hypothesis under the frozen Day 26 exposure definition. The all-future primary contrast was negative, with its 95% bootstrap confidence interval entirely below zero. Pre-specified secondary analyses were broadly consistent with this result: F3 remained negative, the F3 positive-to-negative failure channel showed approximately zero/slightly negative contrast, all three class-conditioned contrasts were negative, the three largest sources were negative, df>=50 sensitivity was more strongly negative, and both unadjusted and adjusted logistic models produced odds ratios below one. Therefore, historical lexical-label instability as operationalized on Day 26 does not explain higher future classification error. This result does not rule out lexical/topic shift more broadly; rather, it separates the Day 26 historical instability construct from the future failure mechanism observed in earlier days.
